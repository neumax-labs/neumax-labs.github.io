How it works

  1. Send the form below. Include your app link and, for a fix, the full rejection text.
  2. We reply in writing confirming what we will do and the timeline, or telling you if we are not the right fit. We aim to reply within 24 hours.
  3. Pay with the secure link for your option (card payment through Stripe). Please pay only after our written confirmation.
  4. Give access to your App Store Connect (see below) and to your project code.
  5. We do the work and report by email. Nothing is submitted to Apple or Google without your written approval.

Refunds. Cancel before work starts: full refund. If we miss the promised deadline (for example, 72 hours for a rejection fix): full refund. After we deliver, there is no refund, but if Apple rejects the app again we fix and resubmit up to two more times at no extra cost. We cannot promise Apple's approval — Apple decides.

Accounts and access

The app is published from your own Apple Developer account, so you own it. Apple's membership fee is paid by you to Apple and is not part of our price. Google charges its own one-time registration fee for a Play Console account.

Invite our team member as Admin in App Store Connect

  1. Sign in to App Store Connect and open Users and Access.
  2. Click + to add a user. First and last name: Kevin Choi. Email: sc@neumaxtech.com.
  3. Role: Admin. Then click Invite.

Why Admin and not App Manager: signing a release build needs a distribution certificate, and App Manager cannot create one. Admin can.

What we will not do in your account: accept or change agreements, tax or banking; make or change payments; change prices without your written request; delete apps, builds or data; or look at your users' personal data. Admin access technically allows some of these, which is why we state it here. You can remove us any time under Users and Access, and we recommend doing so once the work is finished.
